Observation before attribution

Project Magruder

Project Magruder is an ongoing intelligence and research effort examining disruptions to essential infrastructure around the world.

Our methodology begins with observable events, not assumptions about their cause. Significant disruptions are documented, their consequences assessed, and available evidence collected for further analysis.

Cyber-causation, confidence levels, and threat-actor attribution are evaluated separately. Unknown causes do not exclude an incident from examination.

Through longitudinal analysis, we examine recurring infrastructure vulnerabilities, attack techniques, operational patterns, and second-order effects. Additionally, we profile and track recurring attributed threat actors, to establish patterns of methodology -- further aiding in tracking and attribution.

The objective is straightforward: turn verified observations into useful intelligence, and useful intelligence into practical defensive capabilities.

Disclosure note: We use LLM tooling for data capture, bulk research, and some general code generation. All analysis, reports, bulletins, and final software/code releases are the finished work of a living, breathing human that reads and (sometimes) replies to emails.

Research and incident monitoring are ongoing. Project Magruder's reports and technical bulletins will be published in this space routinely. Past reports and bulletins are rolled into a monthly archive and hyperlinked appropriately.

Projects

Active Research

Project Magruder

An ongoing intelligence program monitoring significant disruptions to critical infrastructure worldwide.

The research combines incident tracking, source verification, technical analysis, and longitudinal pattern recognition. Observable events are recorded independently of suspected cyber-causation.

Analysis examines attack methods, tools, operational patterns, infrastructure dependencies, and second-order effects. The objective is to identify meaningful trends over time and develop actionable intelligence as a regular product.

Status: Operational research and monitoring.

In-Progress Software Development

Project Goodfellow

A downloadable mobile application intended to make critical infrastructure threat intelligence accessible to the public.

The application will build upon the research methods and intelligence gathered through Project Magruder.

In development for Android and iOS, the product will emphasize correlated observations, actionable intelligence, and dependable operation.

Status: Pre-release development. Release Date: TBD.

About Chi Rho Development

Research & Development constitutes the beating heart of Chi Rho Labs -- it's not a separate company. We have no interest in joining an endless littany of prematurely-released and perpetually updated software products.

We do our homework so that we bring polished products to market, and then we support them -- an expectation that should never have been cast aside for a quick buck. We do our homework so that our products keep the promises that they make. We do our homework so that cybersecurity and threat-awareness are grounded in understanding and execution, not policy statements and buzzwords.

We're not into reinventing the wheel. We just want things that make sense and function reliably, so we work as practitioners with process -- to build sensible, orderly things that enhance agency. We hope this is a future that you will join us in developing.
